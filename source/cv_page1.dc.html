Research profile

Researcher in metaheuristic computing and stochastic optimisation. My doctoral work developed two enhanced smoothed functional algorithms for data-driven modelling and control, benchmarked against PSO and simulated annealing. Nine selected publications (full list on Scopus/ORCID), three in Q1 journals, six as first author. My problems are black-box, derivative-free optimisation tasks, the same class that search-based software engineering addresses. I am now applying this expertise to software testing, maintenance, security and AI for software engineering.
